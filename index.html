MERIDIAN

ORBITAL FLIGHT SIMULATOR

Meridian 1 is fueled on LC-1 with three crew aboard. Your mission: reach a stable orbit above 140 km, then bring the crew home.

  • Throttle up, then hold IGNITE to launch.
  • Climb vertically to ~1 km, then pitch east (joystick up) gradually — about 45° by 12 km.
  • At apoapsis 200+ km, CUT throttle, coast to AP, then burn PROgrade until PE is above 140 km.
  • To come home: burn RETRO until PE drops to ~30 km, stage the capsule off, hold RETRO, chutes below 7 km.

Drag to orbit the camera, pinch to zoom. Hold the joystick to steer. On the attitude ball: ● prograde, ✕ retrograde.
